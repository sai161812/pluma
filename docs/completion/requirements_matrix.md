Status meanings:

- IMPLEMENTED: the stated narrow invariant is implemented and has the described fresh fixture evidence; broader release acceptance remains separate.
- UNWIRED: component/contract exists but its required production integration is absent.
- UNVERIFIED: implementation/test source exists, but required native, device, complete-path or exact-build evidence is absent.
- DEFECTIVE: a reproduced failing invariant or a concrete source contradiction is identified; inspection-only findings are explicitly distinguished from BA reproductions.
- MISSING: no usable exact registered capability/required implementation located. Related helpers do not establish an accepted alias.
- OPTIONAL: source explicitly makes it optional. Missing mandatory catalog operations are never put here.

Source requirements in the A-J, section-28, catalog, surface, capability, law and build-gate tables are mechanically checked against repository documents. Full text is retained to expose omissions. The checker validates coverage and links, not implementation correctness. A passing suite count, xfail, skip, fake adapter, no-op verifier, rendered gallery or transport `ok` cannot substitute for a requirement's readback evidence.
